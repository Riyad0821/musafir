### The Prophetic Supplication for Joy (দুআ ও ভাবার্থ)

> **« أَضْحَكَ اللَّهُ سِنَّكَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ »**  
> *"Adhakallahu sinnak ya Rasulallah"*  
> **"May Allah always keep you smiling, O Messenger of Allah!"**  
> **"হে আল্লাহর রাসুল! আল্লাহ আপনার মুখে সর্বদা হাসি অটুট রাখুন (আপনাকে চিরকাল সহাস্য ও প্রফুল্ল রাখুন)!"**

When Sayyiduna Umar ibn al-Khattab (رضي الله عنه) entered upon the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) and found him smiling, his spontaneous reaction was not merely a greeting of peace, but an intimate and warm supplication for the continuation of that divine joy upon the beloved Prophet's face.

In classical Arabic rhetoric, the idiom **أَضْحَكَ اللَّهُ سِنَّكَ** (literally: *"May Allah make your teeth smile"*) is an affectionate prayer made when seeing someone delighted or laughing, asking Allah (سُبْحَانَهُ وَتَعَالَىٰ) to perpetuate their happiness, protect them from sorrow, and make their gladness enduring.

### Context & Narrative (প্রেক্ষাপট ও তাৎপর্য)

1. **Prophetic Gentleness with Household & Kin:**  
   The women of Quraish were expressing their domestic grievances and entreating the Prophet (ﷺ) for greater support without reservation, reflecting the supreme accessibility, patience, and tenderness of the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ).
   
2. **The Revering Awe of Umar (رضي الله عنه):**  
   The moment Umar's permission to enter was heard, the women immediately withdrew behind the screen (hijab). The contrast between their informal insistence with the gentle Prophet (ﷺ) and their swift decorum upon hearing Umar (RA) brought a warm smile to the Prophet's face.

3. **Subsequent Exchange in Sahih al-Bukhari 3683:**  
   After the Prophet (ﷺ) conveyed his wonder at how swiftly the women withdrew, Umar addressed them with his characteristic zeal:
   > *"O enemies of yourselves! Do you fear me while you do not fear the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ)?"*  
   > They replied: *"Yes, for you are sterner and harsher than the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ)."*  
   > The Prophet (ﷺ) then said to Umar:  
   > **« وَالَّذِي نَفْسِي بِيَدِهِ، مَا لَقِيَكَ الشَّيْطَانُ سَالِكًا فَجًّا قَطُّ إِلاَّ سَلَكَ فَجًّا غَيْرَ فَجِّكَ »**  
   > *"By the One in Whose Hand is my soul! Never does Satan meet you on a pathway but he takes another path than yours!"*

### Lessons for the Spiritual Traveler (শিক্ষণীয় দিক)

- **Greeting Joy with Supplication (দোয়া ও হৃদ্যতা):** When we see our fellow believers, parents, spouses, or teachers smiling or rejoicing, it is from prophetic etiquette to supplicate that Allah perpetuates their happiness and eases whatever burdens lie behind their smiles.
- **Dignity & Reverence (হিজাব ও গাম্ভীর্য):** The promptness with which the women observed the screen highlights the natural modesty and reverence instilled within the early community of believers.
- **Truth Overpowers Deception (হকের তেজ):** The spiritual fortitude of Umar (RA) was so unyielding upon truth that falsehood and Satan himself could not share a corridor with him.
